# Dog Space (parked)

A dog made of parameters, meant to be shaped by its owner choosing between whole dogs instead of describing them.

**Parked by [Decision 001, Revision 1](../decisions/001-first-build.md).** Two reasons:
- Picking one of six variants per generation can't converge in about 34 dimensions within a human's patience.
- Gallery-based tuning of procedural animation already exists (Brochu et al. 2010; Koyama et al. 2020).

It may return once the footage shows which signals carry a dog's identity. It would then use preference learning on a small basis fitted to real movement.

Only `src/genome.js` (the parameter list and presets) and `src/rng.js` were written.
