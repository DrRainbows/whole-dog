# Handler requests

What a handler can ask for. A request is a goal: "accepts" lists the states that count as done, and the dog chooses among them by its own costs (heat, fatigue, arousal, what it wants next). Trials narrow the set ("strict"). Words map many-to-many onto bodies, so each dog needs its own dictionary from cue to request, with a reliability for each.

Same word, different bodies: "aus" means release the grip, or stop barking. "Hup" means sit to a British gundog and jump in agility. "Down" means lie down, or get off. "Off" means get off the furniture, or take the forepaws off a person. "Up" means forepaws up, or jump onto.
