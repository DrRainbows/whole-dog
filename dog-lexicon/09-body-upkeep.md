# Upkeep: eating, elimination, grooming, sleep, physiology

What the body does to keep itself running. Physiology entries are mostly reflexes (RFX). The dog doesn't choose them, but they limit what it can choose: a panting dog sniffs badly, a lame dog won't gallop.
