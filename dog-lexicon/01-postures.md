# Postures

Held body shapes on the support channel. Tempo and target can turn the same shape into different behaviors; where that happens the entry says so.

| id | body | words | with | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stand | All four feet bearing weight, legs roughly vertical, back level, head at or just above the back line | stand, steh | AHD | WRK REST |
| stand_stacked | Forelegs vertical, hind legs set back so the hocks are vertical, head up, weight even; held still for inspection | stack, show stand | H | WRK |
| stand_forward | Weight shifted over the forelegs, neck raised, ears and tail up, body still and tall | alert | AHD | EXP DEF |
| stand_back | Weight shifted over the hind legs, head low or turned away, ready to retreat | backing off | AHD | FEA |
| stand_braced | Forelegs planted ahead of the body, weight pulled back against the collar or harness, head low | putting on the brakes, planting | H | FEA WRK |
| stand_freeze | Sudden total stillness mid-stance, body rigid, mouth closed, often a fixed stare; comes right before escalating or fleeing | freeze, stiffen | AHD | DEF FEA |
| stand_point | Frozen with head, spine and tail aligned toward a scent or target, often one foreleg lifted and folded; held until released | point, on point | AH | PRY WRK |
| stand_paw_lift | Standing on three legs with one foreleg flexed and held up, not aimed at anything | paw lift | AHD | SOC EXP FEA |
| stand_cower | Legs flexed and body lowered, back rounded, tail tucked, ears back, head low | cower, cringe | AHD | FEA SOC |
| stand_over | Standing over another dog with head or chest across its shoulders, body stiff, forming a T | T-posture, standing over | D | DEF SOC |
| stand_hunched | Back arched up, belly tucked, legs drawn under the body | tucked up | A | RFX EDGE |
| stand_hind | Upright on the hind legs only, forelegs free or folded against the chest | stand up, dance, walk tall | AH | PLY WRK EXP |
| forepaws_up | Hind feet on the ground, forepaws resting on something raised: a person, a counter, a ledge, a sill, a platform | perch, paws up, up, jumping up (on a person), counter-surfing (on a counter); ended by "off" | AH | SOC EXP ING WRK |
| sit | Rump on the ground, hind legs folded evenly with the hocks flat beside the body, forelegs vertical, spine upright | sit, sitz | AHD | WRK REST |
| sit_hip | Sitting rolled onto one hip, the upper hind leg kicked out sideways | puppy sit, lazy sit, sloppy sit | AHD | REST CMF |
| sit_pretty | Balanced on the haunches with the spine vertical and both forelegs lifted and folded at the wrists | beg, sit pretty, Männchen | H | WRK SOC ING |
| sit_prop | Sitting with the forepaws resting on a person's lap or a low ledge | paws on lap | AH | SOC ING |
| sit_slump | Sitting with the spine sagging and the head sinking, eyes heavy | nodding off | AHD | REST |
| platz | Chest and elbows down, forelegs straight ahead and parallel, hind legs folded square underneath with the hocks loaded to spring, head up | platz, down (formal), drop, sphinx | AHD | WRK DEF PRY |
| down_sphinx | Chest down, forelegs ahead, hind legs folded but not loaded; weight settled, head up or chin on the forepaws | down, chin on paws | AHD | REST |
| down_hip | Chest down at the front, hindquarters rolled onto one hip with both hind legs to one side | relaxed down, lazy down | AHD | REST CMF |
| sploot_half | Chest down, one hind leg folded under, the other stretched straight back with the pad up | half sploot, side sploot | A | CMF REST |
| sploot | Belly flat on the floor, both hind legs stretched straight back with the thighs turned so the pads face up, pelvis flat | sploot, frog legs, pancake | A | CMF REST PLY |
| loaf | Chest down with the forelegs folded back under the chest and the hind legs tucked | loaf | A | REST |
| crouch_flat | Belly at or on the ground, forelegs gathered, hind legs loaded, head low and forward in line with the spine, eyes locked on the target | clap, eye, stalking down | AD | PRY WRK |
| crouch_spring | Hind legs bunched under the body, weight back on the haunches, forequarters light; held for a beat before a leap | crouch, gather | AD | PRY PLY |
| cower_flat | Body pressed flat, ears pinned, tail tucked against the belly, head low or turned away, often trembling | cowering, hiding | AHD | FEA |
| lateral | Lying on one side, legs stretched away from the body, head on the ground | flat out, side-lying | AHD | REST CMF |
| curl | Lying curled in a ring, nose tucked to the flank or tail, legs gathered in | donut, curled up | A | REST CMF |
| dorsal | On the back, legs loose in the air, spine curved, head tipped to one side | belly up, dead bug | AH | REST PLY SOC |
| dorsal_appease | Rolled onto the side or back and holding still, upper hind leg lifted to bare the groin, tail tucked, ears back, eyes averted | submissive roll | HD | SOC FEA |
| play_bow | Forelegs and chest dropped with elbows down, hindquarters up with hind legs straight, tail up; quick and bouncy, facing a partner | play bow, bow | HD | PLY SOC |
| stretch_bow | The same shape as play_bow, held slowly with forelegs reaching forward and eyes heavy; after rest, aimed at no one | stretch, downward dog | A | CMF |
| prayer | The same shape as play_bow, held still with no wag; a sign of belly pain | prayer position | A | RFX EDGE |
| head_rest | Lying with the chin on something raised: a bed's edge, a step, a person's foot | chin on the edge | AH | REST SOC |
