# Head, mouth, nose

The head channel: where the dog looks, what its nose is doing, what its mouth holds. When labeling, map face entries to DogFACS action units (Waller et al., 2013) so existing labeled data can be reused.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| orient | Head, then ears, then body turn toward a stimulus and hold | alert, orienting | AHD | EXP PRY |

| head_shake | Fast side-to-side shake of the head, ears flapping | | A | RFX CMF |
| head_turn_away | Head turned away from a person or dog while the body stays | looking away | HD | SOC |
