# Posture graph

How the body gets from one posture to another. A posture is reached only along these edges, never by blending two postures that aren't neighbors. Each edge is its own motion with its own test: transitions are where foot sliding came from.

| from | to | how | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| stand | sit | tuck: hind feet step forward under the body and the rump drops | formal sit; forefeet stay put |
| stand | sit | rock-back: rump drops straight back, forefeet slide back | casual sit |
| sit | stand | kick-back: hind legs push back and straighten, forefeet stay | formal stand (steh) |
| sit | stand | step-up: forelegs walk forward as the rump lifts | casual |
| stand | platz | drop: all four legs fold at once, landing square | down from motion in IGP |
| stand | platz | fold-back: forelegs reach forward and the chest lowers, then the rear folds | slower |
| sit | platz | slide: forelegs walk or slide forward until the chest is down, rump stays | |
| platz | sit | push-up: forelegs push the chest up, rump stays | |
| platz | stand | rear-first or front-first rise | rear-first is the faster, sport version |
| platz | down_sphinx | unload: hocks relax, weight settles | arousal dropping |
| down_sphinx | down_hip | hip roll: hindquarters tip onto one side | |
| down_hip | lateral | shoulder lowers, body rolls onto its side, head goes down | settling to sleep |
| lateral | dorsal | roll onto the back | |
| dorsal | lateral | roll to either side | roll_over continues to the far side |
| lateral | down_sphinx | roll up onto the chest, forelegs come under | |
| dorsal | stand | twist-up: whole-body twist straight onto the feet | fast; in play or when startled |
| down_sphinx | sploot | hind legs swing back and the thighs turn over | cooling, stretching |
| sploot | down_sphinx | hind legs swing forward under the body | |
| sit | sit_pretty | forelegs lift, spine rises to vertical, balance moves onto the haunches | |
| sit_pretty | stand_hind | hind legs straighten to full standing | |
| stand | forepaws_up | rear up onto the hind legs, forepaws land on the support | |
| forepaws_up | stand | push off, forepaws drop to the floor | the "off" request |
| stand | play_bow | fore end drops fast, rear stays up | |
| play_bow | stand | rebound, often straight into a bounce or a sprint | |
| stand | curl | circle 1–3 times, lie down, curl | the bed_down sequence |
| stand | lateral | flop: drops onto its side all at once, with a thud | tired or hot |
| any | fall | slip, stumble, collision, bad landing | see 03 |
| fall | stand | scramble up, often followed by shake_off_dry | |

Not edges: lateral to sit and dorsal to sit (both pass through down_sphinx), sploot to stand without first bringing the hind legs under. Old or sore dogs rise front-first with the hind end lagging; that is a physiology modifier (stiff_rise), not a new edge.
