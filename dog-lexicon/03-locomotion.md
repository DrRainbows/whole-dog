# Locomotion

Ways of moving through space, on the support channel. Gaits are defined by footfall order. Other channels run on top: carrying, sniffing, ears on a sound.

| id | body | words | with | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| walk | Four-beat gait: left hind, left fore, right hind, right fore; two or three feet always down | walk | AHD | EXP REST |
| amble | Fast four-beat lateral walk, between walk and pace | amble | A | EXP |
| pace | Two-beat gait, both legs on one side moving together, body rolling side to side | pace | A | CMF |
| trot | Two-beat diagonal gait, opposite fore and hind moving together | trot | AHD | EXP WRK |
| trot_flying | Trot with a moment where all four feet are off the ground | flying trot, extended trot | A | EXP WRK |
| crab | Trot with the body angled to the line of travel so the hind feet pass beside the forefeet | crabbing, sidewinding | A | EXP |
| canter | Three-beat gait: one hind, then the diagonal pair, then the leading fore, then a suspension | canter, lope | AHD | EXP PLY |
| gallop_transverse | Four-beat run with fore and hind leads on the same side, one suspension | gallop | AHD | PLY PRY FEA |
| gallop_rotary | Four-beat run with footfalls circling the body; sighthounds add a second suspension with the body fully stretched | sprint, flat out | AHD | PRY PLY |
