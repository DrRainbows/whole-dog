# Recipes

Each body entry written as modes (from [11-modules.md](11-modules.md)), manner and target.

Class says what the entry needs beyond that:
- **P** nothing: modes, manner and target are enough.
- **X** physics: the world acting on the parts produces it.
- **R** a reflex: one trigger rule from the body (itch, heat, pain, sleep).
- **D** a policy over time: choosing a place, following a trail, meeting something that moves.
- **O** an estimate of what someone else is attending to or about to do.
- **L** training to a cue.
